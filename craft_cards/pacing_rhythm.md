# Pacing Rhythm

Targets: pacing and transitions, chapter arc

When a scene's tension peaks, cut to the next beat within two sentences of the climax moment. Do not linger to explain what just happened — let the character's next action carry the meaning. The reader processes the impact during the transition, not during a summary paragraph.

In the quiet beat after action, anchor the character in one physical task (mending a strap, counting coins, cleaning a blade) while their mind processes what happened. The hands-busy-mind-working pattern prevents reflective passages from stalling momentum.
