# Domain Strangeness

Targets: originality, sensory immersion, worldbuilding

When the POV character encounters something fantastical, filter the reaction through their professional instincts before their emotional response. A mason notices the impossible joint before feeling dread. A perfumer identifies the absent note before recognizing the enchantment. The expert reaction — the diagnostic reflex — should arrive first and feel automatic, because that is how expertise actually works.

The gap between what the character's training says should be true and what they are observing is where wonder lives. Do not close this gap with explanation. Let it stay open through the scene.
